- Source: Lejuez, Hopko and Hopko (2001). Brief behavioural activation treatment: activity monitoring, then graded scheduling of valued activities. Evidence: moderate.

- The move comes before the mood: the app offers a move at the reading, never waits for the wish to do it.

- Activity monitoring: the record itself, and the counts under Follow-through.

The two chips: "Nothing landed today" is behavioural activation’s "no mastery or pleasure today" in plain words. The answer shows how many times before and what the following days looked like, from the record, never a verdict.

### Self-determination theory

- Source: Ryan and Deci (2000). Autonomy, competence and relatedness as the three needs that carry motivation and wellbeing. Evidence: strong.
